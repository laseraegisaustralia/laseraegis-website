Most commercial RF amplifier suppliers do not offer dedicated military-standard testing, and larger defence primes that do typically require large production volumes and longer lead times. As an engineer-owned, Australian-based Design Authority, LaserAegis offers both — environmental and EMI/EMC testing available on request, with small-batch orders and shorter lead times than conventional supply chains, and no contractor markups.
