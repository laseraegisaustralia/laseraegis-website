RF & Laser Systems Engineering Capability

LaserAegis Australia specialises in the integration, verification, validation, and commissioning of RF and Laser-based systems — acting as the technical lead and Design Authority for the delivered system architecture. Our capability spans two product lines, RF high-power amplifiers and laser systems, supplied to Australian and international customers.

How we deliver

From requirements to supported systems

Each programme follows an agreed engineering scope, from defining the application to acceptance and ongoing support.

  1. 01

    Define

    Agree performance, interfaces, operating conditions and acceptance requirements.

  2. 02

    Develop

    Establish the architecture, technical specifications and component or subsystem selection.

  3. 03

    Integrate

    Coordinate sourcing, partner manufacturing and system integration within the agreed scope.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Assess performance against defined criteria and assemble the applicable acceptance evidence.

  5. 05

    Support

    Provide technical assistance, fault investigation and maintenance coordination as agreed.

Verification & validation

Evidence matched to the delivery scope

We agree test methods, applicable standards and acceptance criteria for the system’s configuration and intended application.

01

How performance is verified

In-house verification
Engineering development, integration and functional or pre-compliance verification are performed in-house.
Formal qualification
Environmental, EMC and other qualification testing is arranged with appropriately qualified specialist or accredited facilities where required by the programme.
02

What accompanies delivery

The documentation package is agreed for each project and can include:

  • RequirementsRequirements specifications, compliance matrices and requirements traceability
  • Interfaces & configurationInterface control and configuration records
  • VerificationVerification cross-reference matrix, plans, procedures and test reports
  • AcceptanceFactory or site acceptance procedures and reports, where applicable
  • Quality recordsInspection records, calibration certificates and certificates of conformity
  • Engineering changesNon-conformance, corrective-action and engineering change records
  • Release documentationRelease and as-built documentation

To keep verification evidence connected, our Requirements Traceability Manager links requirements with implementation, test cases, results and change history.

Supply and export

Supplying Australian and international customers

Our products and supply route are ITAR-free. Enquiries from overseas customers and distributors are welcome.

Export controls

Each export shipment is assessed against Australian export control requirements before dispatch, including the Defence and Strategic Goods List, customs requirements, laser classification, batteries and dangerous goods, and end-user and end-use restrictions.

Shipping

Equipment ships domestically or internationally using controlled commercial freight or courier arrangements appropriate to the equipment.

Installation

Three levels: customer installation with documentation and remote engineering support; LaserAegis-assisted installation; or full installation and commissioning led by LaserAegis engineers.

Technical support

Support beyond delivery

Engineering consultancy and ongoing support for RF and laser systems, including equipment from other manufacturers.

Remote engineering

Initial assessment, remote diagnostics and configuration support.

Technical investigation

Failure analysis, test-data review and root-cause investigation.

Repair & modification

Repair coordination, component replacement, software updates and return-to-service testing.

Field support

On-site installation, commissioning, troubleshooting and acceptance assistance.

We assess documentation, system access and technical scope before recommending work. Response arrangements reflect system criticality, location and the agreed scope.

Warranty terms are specified in the quotation, purchase order or supply agreement. Extended warranty and service arrangements are available for qualifying programmes.
