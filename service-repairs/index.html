Service lines

RF & laser repair and servicing support

01 / Military-grade systems

Military-Grade RF & Laser Systems

Technical assessment, fault investigation, diagnostics, repairs and servicing for military-grade RF amplifiers, laser systems and associated control electronics.

We agree technical information and access requirements to support the assessment and define the work.

02 / RF & electronics

RF Equipment & Electronic Systems

Technical review, fault isolation and servicing support for RF power amplifiers, laser systems, power supplies, protection circuits, control and monitoring electronics, and related assemblies.

RF output and performance measurements are matched to available test capability, documentation and access.

03 / Medical equipment

Medical Laser Equipment

Equipment assessment, investigation of reported faults and servicing support for medical lasers and associated electronics, with repairs considered for the specific equipment.

Where appropriate, we may conduct or arrange an on-premises assessment. Safety, availability and clinical-use requirements are agreed in advance.

Common questions

Service FAQs

Can you assess equipment purchased elsewhere?

Enquiries about other manufacturers’ RF or laser equipment are welcome. Please provide the make, model and fault description. We confirm whether we can provide or arrange support after reviewing the equipment and available technical information.

Can you come to our site, or should we send the equipment?

For medical laser equipment, an on-premises assessment may be conducted or arranged where appropriate. Share your location to discuss the options. Contact us before dispatching equipment; assessment and transport arrangements must be agreed first.

How much will the assessment or repair cost?

Any assessment charges and the proposed repair quotation are agreed before the relevant work begins. Additional work requires your approval. An initial enquiry does not commit you to a repair.

How quickly can the equipment be assessed and repaired?

Tell us if the equipment is out of operation and your preferred timeframe. Availability and timing are confirmed after technical review, taking account of the fault, parts and any specialist work required.
