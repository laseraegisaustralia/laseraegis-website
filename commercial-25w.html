Commercial 25 W RF Power Amplifier Modules

25 W modules in a 117 × 58 × 18.5 mm form factor, 20 MHz to 6.1 GHz. Low-band variants without isolator; mid- and high-band variants with integrated isolator. 28 V DC.

Commercial series: built to order, standard commercial environmental rating, with the same models and electrical specification as the Commodore 25 W series. For field, vehicle-mounted or operational use to MIL-STD requirements, specify Commodore.
