Commercial Series RF Power Amplifier Modules

RF high-power amplifier modules for telecommunications, research and commercial applications, in four power classes from 10 W to 100 W. Same electrical specification as the Commodore™ Series, with a standard commercial environmental rating.

Features

  • Environment Standard commercial environmental rating.
  • Specification Same electrical specification as the Commodore™ Series.
  • Power supply 28 V DC operation.
  • Configurations With or without integrated output isolator, depending on frequency band; RS-485 control on selected 100 W variants.

Applications

  • Telecommunications
  • Research and laboratory systems
  • Commercial RF systems
  • Test and measurement
