Commercial 100 W RF Power Amplifier Modules

100 W modules for research, laboratory and commercial applications, 20 MHz to 4.2 GHz. RS-485 digital control interface available on selected variants. 28 V DC.

Commercial series: built to order, standard commercial environmental rating, with the same models and electrical specification as the Commodore 100 W series. For field, vehicle-mounted or operational use to MIL-STD requirements, specify Commodore.
