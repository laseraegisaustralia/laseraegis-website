Laser Modules

Laser rangefinder modules, illuminators and pointers, and airborne electro-optical payloads, integrated and verified under Australian Design Authority.

Features

  • Wavelengths 905 nm (Class 1) and 1535 nm Erbium Glass (Class I) rangefinders; 830–860 nm illuminator and pointer.
  • Range Rangefinder modules from 400 m to 15,000 m operational range.
  • Interfaces TTL or RS-422 digital interfaces on rangefinder modules.
  • Delivery Sourced from qualified manufacturers and delivered under Australian Design Authority, with compliance and traceability documentation.

Applications

  • UAV and drone integration
  • EO payloads and night vision
  • Thermal scope and handheld systems
  • Defence, fire control and border surveillance
