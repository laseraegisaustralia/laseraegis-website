A local browser interface. Your chosen data location.

Practical questions

Before you start.

Where does the browser connect?

Your browser connects to RTM running on the same computer. For team use, each computer runs its own RTM instance against the shared project data folder. Tested with ten active users. For larger teams, discuss your users, computers and shared-folder environment with us so we can confirm a suitable setup.

Does operation require an internet connection?

RTM supports offline operation and request-code licence activation. Your chosen shared data folder still needs the appropriate network access for team use.

How should we back up project data?

Back up the whole data folder with RTM closed or using an appropriate consistent snapshot, following the manual. Reports and CSV exports are readable outputs, not a full replacement for the project backup.

What if our IT team needs to approve the program?

Contact us for the supplied build and its deployment details. Follow your organisation’s software approval process. Local deployment does not remove your IT approval, shared-folder, access-control or backup requirements.

Do suppliers need an RTM licence to answer?

Suppliers can complete and return the exchange CSV files without running RTM. Your team imports and manages those responses in RTM.

Can we bring requirements from DOORS or another tool?

RTM supports documented ReqIF and CSV import/export workflows. ReqIF import does not bring in attachments, images or baselines, and rich XHTML is reduced to plain text. If a particular exchange file is important to your procurement, discuss a representative sample with us.

Does RTM guarantee compliance with our contract or standard?

RTM supports requirements, change and evidence workflows. The applicable contract, customer acceptance criteria and required outputs still determine what your team must deliver. Tell us if a customer mandates a specific tool or exchange format.

How do we arrange support or initial setup?

Contact the LaserAegis team to discuss activation, initial project setup, training or technical questions. Your quotation will identify the software and service scope.
