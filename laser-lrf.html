Laser Rangefinder (LRF) Modules

Eye-safe laser rangefinder modules spanning 400 m to 15,000 m operational range. 905 nm (Class 1) and 1535 nm Erbium Glass (Class I) wavelengths, with TTL or RS-422 digital interfaces. Operating temperature from −40 °C to +70 °C, depending on model.
