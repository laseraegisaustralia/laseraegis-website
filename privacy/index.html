Privacy Policy

How LaserAegis Australia collects, uses and protects personal information when you use this website or contact us.

1. Acceptance of Terms

Welcome to laseraegis.dev, the website of LaserAegis Australia Pty Ltd (ABN 64 693 917 814), based in Brisbane, Queensland. Your privacy is important to us, and we handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Use of this website is voluntary, and by using it you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. This policy applies only to information collected through this website and through enquiries you send to us.

2. Information We Collect

Non-Personal Information

When you visit the website, we automatically collect general information such as the referring website, browser and device type, screen resolution, approximate location, the time of your visit and the pages you view. This information does not identify you personally. We use it to understand how the website is used and to improve its content and performance.

Personally Identifiable Information

You can browse the website and view product information without providing any personal information. When you submit an enquiry, product request, repairs enquiry or RTM form, or contact us by email or phone, we may collect your name, email address, company, country and the details of your enquiry. By providing this information, you consent to its collection and use as described in this policy.

3. Use of Your Personal Information

We use your personal information to respond to your enquiries, provide datasheets and quotations, supply products and services, and provide repairs and technical support. If you have opted in, we may also send you RTM updates and engineering resources that may be of interest to you.

If you no longer wish to receive marketing emails from us, simply click the “Unsubscribe” link at the bottom of any email we send. If a particular email provides a different way to unsubscribe, please follow the instructions in that email.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with third-party service providers that help us operate the website and respond to you, with supply or servicing partners where needed to fulfil your request, or where required by law. Some of these providers may store information outside Australia, including in the United States.

5. Use of Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your web browser. We use Google Analytics, which uses cookies to help us understand how visitors use the website. If you arrive through a campaign link, the campaign source and landing page are kept in your browser for that session and sent with any enquiry you submit. Embedded YouTube videos may also set cookies when played.

Most browsers accept cookies by default. You can change your browser settings to block or delete cookies, although some parts of the website may not work as intended.

6. Technical and Controlled Information

Please do not send security-classified information, export-controlled technical data or confidential program details through website forms or unencrypted email. Contact us first and we will arrange an appropriate channel.

7. Linked Sites

This website contains links to other websites, such as LinkedIn and YouTube. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites, which are governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any website that collects your personal information.

8. Security Measures

We take reasonable technical and administrative steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we keep it only for as long as needed. However, no transmission of information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and any information you send to us online is sent at your own risk.

9. Reviewing Your Information

To access or update the personal information we hold about you, or to raise any privacy concern, please email xander@laseraegis.dev or call +61 451 986 931.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, so we encourage you to review it periodically. If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at xander@laseraegis.dev.
