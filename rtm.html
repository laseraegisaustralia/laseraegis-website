Can suppliers reply without adopting RTM?

Yes. Suppliers can complete and return the exchange CSV files without running RTM. Your team imports the replies and retains the response rounds, clarifications and decisions in the project record.

Can we bring existing requirements?

Bring existing requirements through CSV or ReqIF exchange. We can help check a representative file and its mappings. Check the exchange scope with a representative file.

Will this replace our whole engineering toolchain?

RTM focuses on requirements, supplier responses, changes and verification records. It supports engineering judgement and review preparation; the applicable contract and customer still define acceptance.
