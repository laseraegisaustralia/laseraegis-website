Commodore Series Ruggedised RF Power Amplifiers

Ruggedised RF high-power amplifiers for defence and operational environments, integrated and verified under Australian Design Authority. The series includes the ARM I HF/VHF amplifier and RF amplifier modules in four power classes from 10 W to 100 W.

Designed and specified to MIL-STD-810F (environmental) and MIL-STD-461D (EMI/EMC). Units are built to order, with environmental and EMI/EMC testing carried out against agreed, model-specific requirements and test reports supplied to meet project needs.
