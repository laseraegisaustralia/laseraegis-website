Commodore 50 W RF Power Amplifier Modules

50 W modules, 20 MHz to 6.1 GHz. Suitable for ground-based and vehicle-mounted applications. All units at 28 V DC.

Available as Commodore A (fixed Class A) or Commodore AB (adjustable Class A/AB), with the same frequency-band options listed below. Confirm the required RF performance and operating conditions for your selected line. Compare A and AB and discuss your selection.

Commodore series: designed and specified to MIL-STD-810F and MIL-STD-461D. Units are built to order, with test reports supplied. The same models are offered in the Commercial 50 W series for standard commercial environments.
