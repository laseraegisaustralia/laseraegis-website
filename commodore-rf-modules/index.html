Commodore 10–100 W RF Power Amplifier Modules

Select from four power classes, with frequency-band models across 20 MHz–7.5 GHz. Each model covers its specified band; the full range is not available from a single module. Standard models operate at 28 V DC.

Choose a power class and frequency band

The following power classes and band options apply to both Commodore A and Commodore AB.

Operating approach

Choose Commodore A or Commodore AB

Both lines offer 10, 25, 50 and 100 W power classes with the same band-specific options across 20 MHz–7.5 GHz. Choose the bias approach that fits your application.

Commodore A

Fixed Class A

Best suited to

Integration
Allow for Class A quiescent power and cooling requirements.

Commodore AB

Enquire about Commodore AB

Each module covers its specified band. Confirm output power, gain, efficiency and distortion for the selected frequency, bias, waveform and cooling conditions.

For selection advice, send your band, output power, waveform, duty cycle, DC supply and cooling constraints. Include linearity targets such as EVM or intermodulation where relevant.

Features

  • Environment Ruggedised for demanding defence and operational conditions; see the environmental specification below.
  • Protection Over-temperature, over-voltage, over-current, and VSWR protection.
  • Power supply 28 V DC operation.
  • RF performance 50 Ω matched input and output.

Applications

  • Communications, EW and radar subsystems
  • Ground-based RF systems
  • Vehicle-mounted defence systems
  • High-power RF with RS-485 control
