Verification scope

What can be verified for RF amplifier systems

RF performance and pre-compliance verification are performed in-house. Formal EMC and environmental qualification is arranged with qualified or accredited facilities where required by the agreed programme scope.

RF performance

  • Frequency and output power
  • Gain and gain flatness
  • Harmonics and spurious emissions
  • Compression
  • VSWR and return loss
  • Efficiency and noise
  • Modulation, CW and pulsed operation
  • Thermal performance

EMC

  • Conducted and radiated emissions
  • Conducted and radiated susceptibility
  • ESD and transient testing where applicable
  • MIL-STD-461 requirements where specified

Environmental

  • Temperature and thermal cycling
  • Humidity
  • Vibration and mechanical shock
  • Altitude
  • Ingress and environmental sealing
  • Thermal stress

Process, documentation and support apply to every programme. See Engineering & Delivery.
