About LaserAegis Australia

Australian engineering —
Clear technical responsibility

Founded in 2023, LaserAegis Australia is an Australian-owned and operated engineering business based in Brisbane. We specialise in RF power amplification, laser systems and systems integration.

Our founder’s technical background spans RF and laser engineering, defence programmes, requirements engineering, and verification and validation. That experience informs how we turn an application need into a system with defined performance and acceptance criteria.

Technical accountability

A local engineering point of contact

We manage systems engineering and customer accountability from Australia. From defining the architecture to reviewing acceptance evidence, LaserAegis retains technical oversight of the agreed delivery scope.

Customers work with us on the system as a whole: how components fit together, how performance will be demonstrated, and how the delivered configuration will be supported.

Specialist supply chain

The right expertise for each system

Specialist domestic and international partners contribute components, manufacturing and test expertise selected for the application. We work with ISO 9001-certified manufacturing partners while maintaining Australian systems engineering and technical accountability.

Partner involvement is defined for each project, with requirements, interfaces and configuration controlled across the delivery team.
