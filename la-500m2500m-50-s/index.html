Overview

Broadband 100 W RF Power from a Single Module

The LA-500M2500M-50-S is a high-power, Class AB solid-state amplifier built on GaN technology, providing continuous broadband coverage from 500 to 2500 MHz. It delivers 100 W of saturated output power and 50 dB of gain from a single 28 V supply.

Rated output is reached with 0 dBm typical input drive, so the amplifier can be driven from a standard signal generator. Current and temperature monitor outputs and a TTL shutdown input are provided on a 9-pin D-sub connector.

Manufactured under an ISO 9001 certified quality management system, with a standard commercial environmental rating.

Integration

Before You Install

Power Supply

Plan a +28 V DC supply (26–30 V) rated for at least 14 A. Typical consumption is 11.5 A at 100 W output and 2.5 A quiescent.

Cooling

Mount the module on an external heat sink sized for continuous operation. Keep the case within −40 °C to +80 °C during operation.

Drive Level

Rated output is reached at 0 dBm typical input. Keep RF input at or below +10 dBm, the maximum input overdrive.
